## A. Executive findings

1. **Recovery is decided mostly by the instance, not by the arm.**
   - Instance identity explains 72 % of the variance in FULL across the 144 dev-eval-2 episodes; arm identity explains 0.2 %.
   - 26 of 36 instances have the same outcome in all four arms.
   - 16 of 30 single-mechanism instances fail in every arm, and low ground friction is 0/20 and one-side weakness 0/8 across all arms.
   - Every arm contrast rests on 1–5 discordant instances. *(Established.)*

2. **"Correct diagnosis → recovery" is not supported, in either direction.**
   - Pooled DS-1 singles: FULL is 18/70 (26 %) when the leading explanation is exactly correct and 17/50 (34 %) when it is wrong.
     Stage 2's own analysis found 41 % vs 30 %.
   - Correct diagnoses do not lead to different tools: the twin screen saturates. In 22/36 A1e6 episodes, ≥3 tools tie at the top
     of the CMA screen.
   - The repair objective, the screen score and the walking robustness checks never target the second verification clause,
     "farther than the faulty robot". 20 of 104 failures walk on ≥80 % of validation seeds but are WORSE/NOT_VERIFIED on displacement.
   - 38 of 78 walking-robustness failures are near misses at 17–19/24 seeds, one to three seeds below the threshold.
   - *(Established for the association and the mechanisms. The size of the tool-choice effect is unresolved.)*

3. **Feedback from repairs is inert for three separate reasons; the update itself is not defective.**
   - **Update arithmetic is correct:** realised posterior shifts match a replay of each update from its recorded predictions
     (Spearman 0.85).
   - **(i) Discriminating predictions come from structures with no mass:** competitor mass median 0.003; a mass-weighted
     disagreement of ≥1 seed in only 5/183 events.
   - **(ii) The posterior is too concentrated to move:** in 76 of 126 events with a modelled runner-up, the log-odds gap exceeded
     the largest shift 8 binary seeds can produce at T = 6 (3.41 nats).
   - **(iii) Most events cannot reach any decision:** 116 of 183 come after the only point in `robust_repair` where a re-screen is
     possible.
   - *(Established; code inspection plus records.)*

4. **Compound diagnosis fails at candidate generation, not at likelihood.**
   - Whenever the true pair was enumerated, it became the leader: G-m v6 2/2 (mass 0.78–1.00); dev-eval 3/3 compositions in each
     v6 arm (mass ≥0.99).
   - It was never enumerated otherwise. Both true primitives survived the 12-particle generic screen in only 3/13 G-m cases.
   - All 5 cases where the open-world reserve led are cases where no pair containing both truths could be formed.
   - The G-m "fail" (7 vs 5 of 13) rests on 3 vs 1 discordant cases. *(Established on small n.)*

5. **Nemotron and experience *do* change consequential decisions; the `deviated` statistic hides it.**
   - **Nemotron:** in A3v5, Nemotron ran 86 experiments after the engine's own stopping rule had fired (31/36 episodes, 12,924
     twin rollouts). This removed the twin warm start in 16/36 episodes and the tool screen in 4.
   - Among the 26 "delegated" A3v5 episodes, the repair sequence still differs from A1e6 in 19.
   - Single episodes show both the best decision (T8: direct `torso_mass` repair → FULL with 53 plant rollouts) and the worst
     (T7: faulty design 0 submitted).
   - **Experience:** the memory's track-record line ("override 1/10 verified; delegate 23/39") is cited by Nemotron as its reason to
     delegate, and A4x deviates 3/36 vs A3v5 10/36 (exact p = 0.065).
   - *(Established mechanism; net outcome effect undetectable.)*

**Prior interpretations this contradicts:**
- "Repair outcomes carried little differential information." This is only true for mass-bearing hypotheses; raw disagreement
  ≥2 seeds occurs in 94/183 events.
- "A4x's repairs are largely the engine's." They are delegated, but on a Nemotron-altered state.
- "Memory is not connected to action." It is, and its main action is to suppress deviation.
- "Nemotron lacks authority." It has a lot of procedural authority; it uses it mostly on diagnosis budget.

**Plausible but unconfirmed:**
- A tool-availability defect (`foot_friction`) contributes to the low-friction failures.
- Plant-CMA designs over-fit their 4 training seeds.

## C. Quantitative forensic results

### F1: why correct diagnosis does not produce recovery

**Diagnosis × FULL, singles (n = 30 per arm, 14 bases; Wilson 95 %)**

| arm | FULL when exactly correct | FULL when wrong |
|---|---|---|
| A1e | 4/18 (0.09–0.45) | 5/12 (0.19–0.68) |
| A1e6 | 6/19 (0.15–0.54) | 2/11 (0.05–0.48) |
| A3v5 | 4/15 (0.11–0.52) | 4/15 (0.11–0.52) |
| A4x | 4/18 (0.09–0.45) | 6/12 (0.25–0.75) |
| pooled (clustered by instance) | 18/70 | 17/50 |

For compositions (6 per arm), an exact correct pair gives FULL 1/3 in every v6 arm.

**Failure categories (all 144 episodes; mutually exclusive, finer than `ds1_report.category`)**

| category | n | validation walking seeds (of 24) |
|---|---|---|
| FULL | 40 | 20–24 |
| walking robustness and displacement both fail | 41 | 14 at 0; 14 at 17–19 |
| walking robustness only (displacement verified) | 37 | 24 at 17–19 |
| walks ≥80 % but not farther than faulty (WORSE 15, NOT_VERIFIED 5) | 20 | 20–24 |
| 20 s confirmation | 6 | 20–24 |
| error | 0 | – |

**The engine knows when it is failing.**

| mapped P(FULL) of the submitted design | FULL / submitted (v6 arms) |
|---|---|
| < 0.25 | 0 / 22 (all 15 displacement-only failures are here) |
| 0.25–0.5 | 3 / 3 |
| 0.5–0.75 | 7 / 14 |
| ≥ 0.75 | 18 / 22 |

- Only 9–12 of 30 single episodes per arm ever produced *any* checked design with P ≥ 0.5.
- Non-submitted designs never had better plant evidence than the submitted one in a material case. In 3 episodes the alternative
  was also hopeless.

**Conclusion on H5:** stopping and final selection are a minor loss. The dominant loss is that the search never finds a design
that the evidence rates as good.

**Instance profile:**
- Low ground friction 0/20; one-side weakness 0/8; pitch_sensor_bias 15/36; clock 5/8.
- Mild severity (sev 0, ×1.0) 15/40; sev 2 ×1.7 4/32.

**Same instance, different arms:**
- Among (instance, submitted tool) groups with ≥2 arms, the outcome is consistent in 33 and conflicting in 2.
- With the same first tool as A1e6, outcomes agree in 52/56 pairs. With a different first tool, they agree in 44/52.

**Trajectory sensitivity (T2):** same diagnosis, same first tool and same twin 8/8 still split 15/16 vs 12/16 on the plant, and
the verdict hinged on one validation seed.

**Twin → plant transfer:**
- Twin warm starts at ≥7/8 in the twin reach ≥7/8 on the plant in 44/75 cases; the twin over-predicts by ≥3 seeds in 31 %.
- Plant-CMA designs with 4/4 training walking reach r16 ≥ 15 in 52/138 steps.
- Robust (r16 ≥ 15) submitted designs verify 15/24 when they come from the twin warm start and 11/35 when they come from plant
  CMA. 12 of the plant-CMA failures fail on walking robustness. This is confounded by instance difficulty.

**Tool availability (code defect, `belief_engine.screen_tools`):**
- Fixed probe magnitudes that raise an exception are silently skipped. When all probes of a tool fail, the tool disappears from the
  ranking, and every arm then uses only ranked tools.
- With the guided bound `foot_friction ≤ 2.0` (`morphology.BOUNDS`), `foot_friction` is absent in **23/36** instances (every base
  with friction >1.0). This includes all 5 single low-friction instances, even at diagnosis mass 1.00 (T3).
- `torque_limit` is absent in 12/36 and `foot_length` in 2/36.
- **Counter-evidence:** on a stream base where `foot_friction` was available, it ranked first and still failed (T3b). The
  counterfactual on 0073/0126/0137/0096 is **unavailable**.

**Counterfactual status of the missed recoveries (from the 10 mixed-outcome instances):**
- 2 are "directly observed under comparable conditions": same diagnosis and first tool, differing in the second tool or optimizer
  trajectory (T2; 0142:short_feet:2).
- 8 are "suggestive but confounded": the diagnosis, budget path or Nemotron action also differ.
- None is clean evidence that a different tool *choice* alone would have recovered the instance.

| skipped for twin budget (A1e6 / A3v5 / A4x) | 11 / 50 / 37 |
| leader is the unmodelled reserve | 12 / 183 |

**Code facts (`belief_engine.py`):**
- `robust_repair` checks for a leader change only once, after the twin warm-start check (lines 952–960). Assimilations after plant
  optimisation have no path to any decision, because submission uses mapped P(FULL), not the belief.
- A Nemotron `tool_order` disables both the CMA screen and the re-screen.
- The unmodelled reserve inherits the best particle's check likelihood (`unmodelled_mass`, line 396), so repair checks can never
  lower it.
- Tempering divides check evidence by 6, like all evidence. A T = 1 replay gives no more leader changes.

**Classification:** (1) non-discriminating predictions *among mass-bearing hypotheses* is primary; (2) an update defect is
refuted; (3) a missing decision path is co-primary. Belief inertia is real, but it is the result of a concentrated post-diagnosis
posterior, not of tempering.

**Reading the funnel:**
- Nemotron's authority is large at stages 1–3 and almost invisible at stage 5.
- Overriding the stopping rule is the main lever. Its cost is twin budget: assimilation skips, missing warm starts and missing
  screens.
- Yet episodes with post-stop experiments recover 6/31, exactly as A1e6 does on the same instances (6/31). Missing warm starts
  recover 3/16 vs A1e6's 2/16.
- **Resource displacement is real procedurally and undetectable in outcome**, consistent with finding 1.
- **No difference is causally attributable to Nemotron alone.** Every discordant pair also differs in diagnosis path or budget.

## E. Confounding and validity

- **Power and dependence:**
  - 30 singles on 14 bases (1–4 instances each); 6 compositions on 5 bases; the stream covers 7 bases.
  - Paired discordants: A3v5 vs A1e6 2/2 (singles); A4x vs A1e6 3/1 (exact two-sided p = 0.625); A1e6 vs A1e 1/2;
    stream 2/2 (p = 1.0).
  - Base-bootstrap intervals are descriptive only. The apparent A4x − A3v5 interval [0, +6] rests on a single contributing base
    and should be ignored.
  - The 183 assimilations come from 80 episodes, so every distribution above is reported per event and per episode (episode max
    TV: median 0.004; ≥0.1 in 13/80).
- **Selection bias:**
  - R-head rows exist only for tools some procedure chose, and alternatives were scored with weaker procedures.
  - The diagnosis × FULL tables are observational.
  - The track record shown to Nemotron mixes agent versions.
- **Evaluator artifacts:**
  - The displacement clause compares against a faulty robot that can travel far without "walking" (low friction). This is a
    pre-declared criterion, not a defect, but it may penalise stabilising repairs.
  - The near-threshold mass at 17–19/24 makes FULL a high-variance outcome.
- **Budget:**
  - Nominal limits are equal, but realised twin use differs: median A1e 1,888; A1e6 2,558; A3v5 2,773; A4x 2,649.
  - Plant use is ~equal (688–692).
  - A1e leaves ~900 twin rollouts unused. A1e6 vs A1e is therefore also a budget-use contrast.
- **Stochasticity:**
  - Non-LLM arms are deterministic: the A4x no-scientist run reproduces A1e6.
  - All LLM-arm variation is LLM-induced trajectory change. Round 0 of the stream measures it at about the size of every later
    difference.
- **Possible bookkeeping defect:**
  - `_register` stops updating particles when the twin budget runs out, which could leave particles scored on different experiment
    sets.
  - Observed in 4 A3v5 episodes. In 1 (0142:pitch_sensor_bias:5) the posterior changed, so a partial update is possible. In the
    others the plant experiments were spent with no inference.

## F. Outstanding questions (ranked by consequence)

1. **Is a robot that walks but is "not farther than faulty" a genuine repair failure, or does the comparison penalise
   stabilisation?**
   - Needs per-seed validation scores of faulty and candidate. They are not in the episode records; they might exist in VM-side
     verification logs.
   - If not, it needs a controlled re-verification run (simulation; owner approval).
2. **On all-fail instances, does any tool succeed under an equal optimisation budget?**
   - This separates infeasibility from search failure. It needs a controlled development experiment (tool × instance grid) and
     cannot be answered from existing records.
3. **Do robust-seed checks use the validation variation preset?**
   - This decides whether the near-miss gap is a condition mismatch or over-fitting. Answerable by code audit
     (`hdb_workbench.robustness_check`, `hdb_evidence`).
4. **What is LLM run-to-run outcome variance per instance?**
   - Needed before any Nemotron claim. Needs repeated runs (paid). The existing round-0 pairs give only 5 points.
5. **Would compound pairs be identified if enumerated more widely?**
   - Needs re-running diagnosis with a wider keep set (simulation). Per-particle predictions were not stored, so offline replay is
     impossible.
6. **How many of the tool-absent instances would the missing tool recover?**
   - Needs simulation. A code audit can at least list every tool/base affected (done for dev-eval-2).

## G. Recommended next information-gathering action

**First: a code and metric correctness audit (read-only, no cost).** Scope:
- The robust-check vs validation conditions (Q3).
- The `screen_tools` tool-dropping behaviour and its prevalence across *all* development records, not just dev-eval-2.

**Expected information:** high relative to cost. It may turn bottlenecks 2–4 from "plausible" into "established" or "refuted"
without any simulation, and it fixes the measurement basis before any experiment is designed.

**Second, only with owner approval: a small controlled development experiment.** Fixed instances, equal budgets, a tool × seed
replication on the 16 all-fail plus 10 mixed single instances, non-LLM.

**What it would answer:**
- It separates physical infeasibility, tool choice and optimizer variance — the largest unresolved question behind finding 1.
- Its outcome determines whether any diagnosis, Nemotron or memory improvement *can* move FULL on this benchmark.

**Uncertainty:** the experiment may show that most all-fail instances stay unrecoverable under every tool, and that would itself
be the decisive result.
