# DI-1 decision — NOT EVALUABLE / NO-GO

25 October 2026. The engineering pilot collected the missing predecision
counterfactual evidence, but it does not establish the advancement claim.
Do not build an experience-guided investigation mechanism from this evidence.

Five eligible independent bases from eight frozen new local morphology draws
produced 11 distinct final designs. V1 first-probe alternatives reached only one
base; all three policies recovered 3/5 FULL. A prospectively documented uniform
engineering amendment tested stop-or-probe on the same five bases: original
3/5, raw EIG 3/5, random 4/5. Both versions remain reported; repeated paths and
verifier seeds are not independent robots.

One random-probe allocation path rescued base 003 to independently verified
FULL at the original 700 plant / 2,800 twin caps. Raw EIG reached PARTIAL there;
it did not predict the rescue. The random floor probe also worsened base 000.
The successful path includes changed investigation, posterior, resource
allocation and repair; the floor measurement alone has not been isolated as
the cause. Final FULL was evaluated on the original fault/conditions.

The gate definition was not weakened. **NOT EVALUABLE** follows from insufficient
precision and infeasible powered launch, as required by the mission/protocol.
The pilot cannot PASS or FAIL a prospective gate. No main evaluation occurred,
and no fitted learning mechanism was tested. This does not prove that experience
learning is ineffective or that unresolved repairs are physically impossible.
Binomial references and power forecasts assume a working population model that
these fixed family strata/qualification exclusions do not validate; they are
not confirmatory population intervals or universal minimum resource claims.

Potentially useful observable signals are now retained: initial/probe physical
responses and contact/stability, twin response uncertainty, posterior weights,
stopping state, candidate availability and remaining budget. None has established
predictive value beyond random here. No hidden family labels, hindsight FULL
outcomes, entropy thresholds or favorable subgroups became policy inputs.
No history-ignore/applicability learner or Nemotron adaptation was implemented.

The next **separately authorized and preregistered** question should test whether
observable model misfit or early stopping predicts useful extra investigation
on broader independently generated development bases. It needs disjoint training
and evaluation bases, prospective applicability criteria, an uninformative or
shuffled-information control, charged ranking/probe/search costs, original FULL
and whole-base uncertainty. First establish a defensible efficient measurement
design and powered runtime; the present full-cap three-path design does not fit
the existing resource bounds. One successful floor path does not justify an
always-floor policy or a post-hoc stopping threshold. No such follow-up, learner,
R1 or sealed evaluation is initiated by this decision.
