# VEL-1 decision — INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE

26 October 2026. **Do not advance to implementing/deploying a complete
experience-guided verification-risk system.** A real preverification failure
target exists, but the incremental experience benefit, unfamiliar-mechanism
transfer and useful warning-contingent recovery action remain unestablished.

This is the mission's **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE** conclusion. It is not a generic
NO-GO claim that there is little predictive headroom: physical/state information
does improve on raw walking confidence and the older stop map. It is also not
GO: the simpler-calibration and relevant-history comparisons do not satisfy the
fixed feasibility screens. No prospective experiment or full architecture is
approved or initiated by this report.

## Smallest meaningful next measurements, if separately requested

First resolve the specific repeatability gap rather than build a bigger agent.
An economical diagnostic panel could select **one R0 apparent-success candidate
per supported base** (15 bases have select8≥7) by a newly frozen hash rule using
preverification evidence, without conditioning on its FULL result. Evaluate the
same exact candidates on **two new, disjoint full-verifier seed panels**. Preserve
all requirements and simulator errors. This is at most15×2×96=2, 880 rollout
equivalents, with no new policy optimization. It can reveal label flips and
requirement consistency on these known cases; it cannot establish transfer,
representative failure prevalence or a learning effect. R0's original cohort
was selected from historically difficult/control cases, so that selection remains
a limitation. Such repeats have not been run in VEL-1.

Using the measured DI-1 six-worker path throughput as a rough throughput reference,
that physics is about 0.015 batch hours; setup/retrieval dominates. A separately
bounded15–30 minute existing-VM window would budget roughly$0.53–$1.06 plus storage
and operational margin at the recorded$2.1169/hour. This is a proposal, not a
current balance check, committed reservation or permission to start. Fresh credit,
an explicit native deadline/watchdog and API-stop verification would be required.

For the broader data gap, a **30-new-base diagnostic development panel** with
two independently budgeted paths/base, two verifier panels per exact submitted
candidate, complete precommitment physical logs and no historical base-lineage
reuse would improve provenance and variance estimates. Freeze train/evaluation
roles and the entire attempt range first. This is not enough to certify a small
experience effect; do not describe it as a powered advancement trial. Under
DI-1's conservative measured throughput, 25% runtime margin and qualification
reference, its maximum-cap physics forecasts roughly1.19 batch hours/$2.51,
before setup/retrieval/unexpected exclusions. With operational allowance it is
an order-of-magnitude$4–$6 diagnostic proposal, not an authorized VEL-1 run.

These panels answer different missing questions; neither automatically licenses
experience implementation or a sealed study. The lowest-cost repeatability
panel is a sensible first measurement only if pursuing the direction remains
worthwhile to the owner.

## Scale of a later independent test

Existing utility fitting/replay takes seconds to tens of seconds on a single
CPU thread. No foundation-model training or GPU is justified by this feasibility
result. Engineering work would mainly involve preserving precommitment evidence,
independent grouped calibration and a charged warning-action protocol; no full
implementation architecture is recommended before those gaps are resolved.

For a sensitivity reference, 17 apparent-success base-level V2−V1 loss differences
have SD.04419. An illustrative normal paired calculation for a useful.005 Brier
increment gives approximately614 independent bases for 80% power or 821 for 90%,
two-sided5%. It is **not** a validated future-design minimum, and fitted OOF
residual variation/17 historical bodies cannot establish future sampling
variance. Other mechanisms/effects may differ; a pilot should support a new power
calculation rather than spending to this number blindly.

At614 new bases, a two-arm700 plant/2800 twin design with maximum96 verifier
equivalents/path, eight concurrent six-worker paths and 25% runtime margin would
forecast about 22.7 VM hours/$48.1 **before qualification and other overhead**;
three arms34.1 hours/$72.1. These use the recorded batch quote and DI-1 throughput,
not a fresh capacity reservation. This predicts the cost of collecting evidence,
not the cost of the tiny statistical model. They also omit acquisition of enough
apparent-success bases and new training data. One episode/base has different
variance from this panel's multiple episodes/base. Brier power does not establish power
for FULL improvement; an actual recovery study needs its own paired binary
effect/discordance design. No expensive campaign was launched.
