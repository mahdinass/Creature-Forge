# VEL-2 data, timing and lineage audit

Stage A uses only explicit, checksum-verified R0 development evidence. The
original R0 population contains 18 historical development lineages; its 324
policy/optimizer records reduce to320 distinct candidate/verification identities.
The select8>=7 apparent-success condition supports15 base groups. VEL-2 gives
each unique physical candidate one hash ticket and chooses one per base without
reading verification outcomes. Hash aliases do not increase selection weight.
The selected15 nominal-body hashes are distinct. These are **15 known lineages,
zero newly generated lineages**, not45 independent robots or1,440 robots.

## Accessible and evaluator-only information

| Information | Timing / scope |

| New paired validation responses and conditional confirmation | Two new target panels; never predecision predictors |
| Candidate-specific independent improvement measures | New verifier measures are future targets; Stage A creates no new permissible predecision improvement feature |

R0's original apparent-success event is select8>=7, whereas VEL-1's main event
was selected robust16>=15. These populations and search policies are not pooled
or advertised as interchangeable. Known-base enrichment, historical difficult/
control selection and hashed one-candidate sampling limit external inference.

Each new panel has24 matched original/candidate validation seeds and24 distinct
confirmation seeds reserved. The1,440 new seed IDs are mutually disjoint across
all15 bases/panels/roles and disjoint from every original R0 seed role checked
during preparation. Matching original/candidate validation seeds is intentional.
Confirmation is conditional under the existing full verifier; nonexecution is
missing, not failure. Simulator errors cannot be training labels. No new paid
model input, outcome-dependent policy, resampling, threshold search or sealed
dataset access occurs.

## Stage B boundary

Stage B requires30 genuinely new lineages, not DI-1's ±3% variants of historical
parents. The established broad generator `creature_gen.sample_body` samples
from GEN_DIST without a historical parent; `hdb_walkers.py` historically repairs
generated failing bodies at2,000 rollouts to obtain healthy nominal bases. The
earlier VEL-1 cost sketch omitted that extra healthy-base creation burden and
its yield uncertainty. A cheaper direct healthy qualification procedure would
need its own frozen attempt stream, qualification accounting and adequate yield;
no such yield has been measured or assumed here. Stage A's gate is evaluated
before any decision to freeze/start Stage B. Failure of that gate cannot be
overridden by a favorable cost estimate or a newly selected stable subgroup.

No Stage A observation establishes physical/state predictive accuracy, additional
memory value, history relevance or an action effect. The VEL-1 metrics remain
historical evidence, not a new VEL-2 comparison. A probability/risk target across
repeat panels is different from a stable deterministic case label and would
require a separately preregistered design rather than relabelling this one.
