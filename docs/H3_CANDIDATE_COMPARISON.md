# H3 competing proposals — 26 October 2026

Ranks reflect identification strength, credible recovery potential, engineering/evaluation cost and October30 feasibility. They are judgments, not estimated probabilities of success. None is validated by historical negative studies.

| Rank | Proposal | Credibility / gain potential | Contribution / implementation | Evaluation and deadline risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nested asymmetric stance residual plus measured diagnostics | Moderate physical plausibility; benefit unknown | Small missing capability, interpretable class ablation; no algorithmic novelty claim | Cheap isolated pilot possible; risk of one-base anecdote controlled by fresh lineages and repeats |

## 1. Asymmetric stance residual — selected engineering test

**Hypothesis:** a two-parameter left/right stance-demand basis increases repeated FULL recovery under unilateral torque loss beyond tuning the current17D controller, including a double-budget current-family control. **Mechanism:** bounded signed hip/ankle stance bumps on top of existing FP, with zero-gain identity and unchanged actuator clipping. **Evidence:** source confirms the parameterization is absent; older M1 supports testing controller classes; one hard H1 lineage is suggestive only, while RL1's26/34 unilateral recoveries argue against a universal missing capability. **Changes:** research wrapper, scoped plant bridge, matched CMA runner, telemetry and grouped analysis; no release registry change. **Data/adaptation:** no cross-robot training; conventional per-robot CMA adaptation from four training seeds and eight selection seeds; independent physical verification remains decisive. **Cost:** proposed bounded12-lineage pilot, ≤311,808 ten-second equivalents, Cloud ≤$8/3 batch hours, Token Factory$0, subject to fresh approval and throughput gate. **Strongest baseline:** matched17D at3,000 rollouts and17D at6,000; compare body-fixed controllers, not entire A1 system. **Failures:** higher-dimensional search, weak actuator clipping away residual, wrong phase basis, traction-limited or infeasible body, healthy regression. **Falsification:** no meaningful/reliable gain over double-budget17D, or guardrail failure. **Advance:** adds a testable engineering action; no AI-learning claim.

## 2. Search / reliability-aware optimization

**Hypothesis:** existing controllers can already recover the relevant robots; budgets or noisy train/select scores hide good parameters. **Mechanism:** stronger conventional search, restarts or repeated-seed selection, preserving the class. **Evidence:** R0 technical variability, H1 label instability and M1 nonmonotonic checkpoint results; recorded R0 searches did not restart. These do not prove optimizer inadequacy. **Changes:** reuse CMA/evaluation hooks; explicitly charge repeated panels and any changed objective. **Data:** no external history; more per-robot physical trials. **Cost:** double-budget arm alone144,000 pilot training/selection rollouts; additional reliability tuning requires a separately frozen budget. **Baseline:** current17D at equal total physical budget, and selected richer class with the same search kernel. **Failures:** same structural limit, overfitting seed panels, less search coverage when repetitions consume budget. **Falsification:** extended search has no reliable improvement and richer class retains a gain under matched budgets. **Advance:** could improve engineering efficiency; is not learned experience transfer. This proposal is embedded as the selected experiment's strongest control rather than discarded.

## 3. Delay-aware observer/controller

**Hypothesis:** actual buffered state latency causes a phase-lag deficit not removable by current gain/frequency tuning. **Mechanism:** constrained state extrapolation or an identified predictor, with bounded horizon and fallback on poor model fit. **Evidence:** `PlantController` genuinely delays joint/pitch state; published E1 diagnosis often exceeds repair. No inspected legal development set establishes the relevant delay magnitudes or a compensation benefit. **Changes:** sensor adapter, observer validation and mismatch monitoring; preserve distinct undelayed contacts. **Data:** controlled latency identification and held-out conditions, never privileged hidden-delay inputs at decision time. Reserved E1 families require a newly authorized protocol and fresh data. **Cost:** not responsibly priced without qualification/telemetry; at least multiple independent lineages and repeated panels, no present spending request. **Baseline:** current17D, stronger current tuning and simple linear extrapolation at equal budget. **Failures:** differentiation noise, contact discontinuities, unstable extrapolation, biased/incorrect delay estimate. **Falsification:** oracle delay compensation lacks gain, or a learned estimator fails to beat simple physical prediction. **Advance:** potentially addresses a real missing mechanism, presently unsupported as the first project.

## 4. Capability selector / morphology-conditioned adaptation

**Hypothesis:** predecision observations identify which verified family will work, and learned mapping improves decisions beyond fixed/physical selectors. **Mechanism:** begin with a small calibrated family-applicability model or contextual selection rule; consider RMA-like adaptation only after sufficient population/data. **Evidence:** related literature supports possibility; H1, VEL1 and RL1 provide no successful transfer demonstration. Existing warm starts, rankers and dossiers rule out novelty by renaming memory. **Changes:** capability registry, consistent evaluator support, training/fold provenance and frozen selector. **Data:** several useful families, repeated counterfactual outcomes, independent train/test lineages and sham histories if experience is claimed. Eighteen repeatedly measured bases are insufficient. **Cost:** requires a prospectively costed data-acquisition/repertoire campaign; neural simulation training may dominate and is not authorized. **Baseline:** best fixed family, equal-budget physical-only and random family selection, same action space for learned/sham variants. **Failures:** lineage leakage, post-outcome features, unreliable labels, all gains caused by richer actions. **Falsification:** learning fails to exceed these baselines with a preregistered positive clustered interval and practical gain. **Advance:** could become an AI-learning contribution later; building it now would be speculative.
