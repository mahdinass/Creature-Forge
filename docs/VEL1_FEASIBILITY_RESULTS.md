# VEL-1 feasibility results

26 October 2026. **Verdict: INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE for experience-guided
verification-risk learning and a useful intervention.** The failure target is
identifiable and moderately predictable in grouped historical analysis, but
incremental value from relevant episodic history and improved FULL recovery are
not demonstrated. This is not proof learning is impossible or a reason to deploy
the feasibility model. See [decision](VEL1_DECISION.md),
[data/timing audit](VEL1_DATA_AUDIT.md), and frozen [plan](VEL1_FEASIBILITY_PLAN.md).

## Evaluation and what was frozen

The primary cohort has211 submissions on 18 fresh development bodies. Apparent
success (selected robust16≥15, fixed before fitting) gives 115 submissions on 17
bodies, 27 subsequent non-FULL failures on 14 bodies and 88 FULL outcomes. Those
17 base groups, not115 episodes or their seeds, determine primary precision.

Models fit all valid outer-training submissions and score both all submissions
and the frozen apparent subset. Features/hyperparameters are fixed:16 permitted
preverification features, median imputation/missing indicators and scaling inside
training folds, base-equal ridge-logistic fitting with lambda 1. V1a uses only
robust fraction/check size. V2 adds a shrunken five-nearest-training-base residual
memory, whose residuals are generated by inner base-blocked cross-fitting. V3
shuffles the physical-experience/residual pairing between training-base
representatives with 20 fixed seeds. No test-base outcome can enter any training
model, imputation, normalization, memory or threshold.

These are grouped offline histories, not a recovered real-world chronology or
an online Nemotron adaptation. V1 itself learns from historical labels; V2 tests
the incremental value of this particular local residual-memory mechanism.
No fitted model was connected to the engine or used to select a new repair.

Metrics below weight bases equally, with episodes equal within a base. Paired
uncertainty resamples whole bases5, 000 times using the frozen seed. Overlapping
training folds and a small historical development panel limit interpretation;
these are descriptive feasibility intervals, not confirmatory population tests.
They are not simultaneous intervals adjusted for the several comparisons.

probabilities of FULL; the target includes improvement and longer confirmation.

Neither experience contrast meets the fixed.005 useful-increment screen or
positive paired evidence. No alternate neighbor count, shrinkage, feature set,
shuffle or subgroup was selected after these results. This rejects advancement
of the tested memory mechanism on current evidence; it is not a statistically
powered null for all forms of experience.

Family sensitivity excludes both the test family and test physical base from
label training, preventing same-robot leakage through another mechanism. On the
apparent subset V1a/V1/V2 Brier becomes 0.2036/.1847/.1938 and V1 AUROC.6439.
V1's gain over V1a is 0.0189, 95[−0.0092, 0.0469]. V2 log loss worsens to.7609 versus
V1.5729, consistent with some confident wrong residual corrections.

Results are heterogeneous: V1 is worse than V1a for one-side weakness and short
feet; V2 is worse than V1 for most family slices. Slices are small and descriptive,
not individually selected hypothesis tests. The existing twin hypothesis library
still includes physical models for these families. Leaving out risk labels does
not establish generalization to physics absent from the engine's model library.
The frozen original stopping map and resulting accept flag also retain their
legacy family experience. This is blocked primary risk-label sensitivity, not
removal of every source of family knowledge from the complete engine.

## Physical signals, calibration and false alarms

Permitted information contains more than walking counts, but individual feature
contributions were not isolated by a new selected ablation. Exported outer-fold
coefficients give association diagnostics: initial damaged-plant slip and ankle
saturation and the same-design twin/plant gap have positive standardized
coefficients in all 18 folds; robust/stress/train walking have negative ones in
all 18. These correlated, regularized coefficients do not prove causal warning
mechanisms or incremental transfer of each feature. Missingness follows original
probe eligibility/budget, so can itself encode policy selection. Preverification
same-design twin/gap is missing on 152/211 submissions.

Diagnostic confidence alone is weak here and should not be relabelled as repair
reliability. The improved combination is compatible with ordinary calibration
and physical context, without evidence that local memory provides extra useful
information. Lower five-bin ECE for V2 does not offset its lack of Brier benefit,
loss of discrimination or family-shift log-loss degradation. Small sample ECE
is not a risk guarantee.

At the fixed risk-warning threshold 0.5, V1 precision is 55.8%, recall 21.7% and
false-alarm rate among actual FULL outcomes5.7%. V2 precision 51.8%, recall 28.0%,
false alarms8.6%; under base/family exclusion V2 precision falls to33.3%, recall
16.2% and false alarms10.7%. Thus apparently plausible warnings still miss many
failures and can suppress valid repairs. No alarm threshold was optimized.

For V1, retaining predictions below risk 0.25 covers 35.0% of the apparent panel
with 10.0% observed failure risk, while rejecting58.2% of actual FULL outcomes.
Under family exclusion that threshold covers 37.9% with 21.4% failure risk and
rejects60.4% of FULL outcomes. Descriptive80% rank coverage gives 20.1% risk in
base-out and 18.1% in dual-blocked evaluation, versus 24.8% at full coverage.
These curves describe a tradeoff, not independent guarantees or new decision
rules. V1's lowest-risk 0.1 bin is tiny and unreliable under family shift.

## Actionability and information still missing

Abstention could suppress a provisional success interpretation or report
uncertainty. It does **not** increase walking or FULL. Existing deterministic
verification already withholds FULL from failed candidates. Replacing it with
a classifier would weaken the evidence standard and is not recommended.

A warning could instead trigger additional charged robustness evidence,
continued diagnosis, a different candidate or a cost-aware stop. However:

- EM-1 has480 unverified alternative designs. No risk threshold can turn them
  into known successful counterfactual submissions.
- RL-1 covers fixed single-tool repairs at 300 rollouts, not the original700/
  2800-cap pipeline's alternatives or response to a warning.
- R0 covers different, 3000-rollout search allocations on outcome-enriched
  failed/control cases. Its alternatives do not identify a700-rollout warning
  policy effect, and overlap the same18 primary bodies.
- DI-1 gives one random allocation rescue on five local perturbed bodies;
  raw-EIG did not exploit it predictively. It cannot estimate a VEL-1 response
  policy or experience benefit.

No warning-contingent equal-budget recovery gain is identifiable from these
records. Each exact candidate generally has one fixed verifier panel; repeated
indices/same-seed evaluations are not independent reliability labels. Separate
fresh seed panels are needed to estimate across-verification variability. New
decision-time candidate stability and baseline-relative improvement evidence
would help distinguish the predominant validation-improvement failures from
the much smaller longer-confirmation failure subset.

## Focused primary-source literature positioning

This is a focused positioning review, not a claim of exhaustive novelty search.

- **ReAP (Liu et al., 2026)** learns a visual failure representation and predicts
  future risk through an action-conditioned latent transition. Its recognition
  and prediction signals address monitoring timeliness. VEL-1 studies a different
  target: whether a submitted repair passes independent multi-requirement,
  longer-horizon verification. An evidence-only risk audit is not already ReAP's
  action-conditioned monitor. Prospective candidate/action conditioning and
  history relevance would need their own tests. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.16309v1).
- **SAFE (Gu et al., 2025)** learns failure signals from generalist policy
  representations using successful and failed executions, with unseen-task
  evaluation. Generic failure learning/transfer is established prior work;
  Creature Forge cannot claim it as new because of Nemotron. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09937).
- **FIPER (Römer et al., 2025)** uses observation novelty and action-generation
  uncertainty calibrated with successful rollouts, without needing failure
  examples. It motivates strong non-episodic reliability comparisons and
  explicit false alarms, rather than assuming more failure memory must help.
  [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.09459).
- **SelectiveNet (Geifman and El-Yaniv, 2019)** formalizes learning with rejection
  and risk/coverage tradeoffs. Abstaining from a repair claim is a selective
  prediction outcome, distinct from repairing the robot. [Primary paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/geifman19a.html).
- **Guo et al.(2017)** distinguish confidence from empirical correctness and
  evaluate post-hoc calibration. A calibrated robustness-only reference must
  precede claims for a more complex memory model. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.04599).
- **Conformal Risk Control (Angelopoulos et al.)** treats calibration for bounded
  expected loss under specified assumptions. Correlated episodes from a few
  robot bodies cannot simply be called exchangeable independent calibration
  examples, especially under family shift. VEL-1 makes no conformal guarantee.
  [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02814).
- **ReSYNC (Li et al., 2026)** learns recovery skills and planning abstractions
  from failure-recovery experience. Learning from physical failure already has
  substantial prior art; a different verifier or memory backend alone is not
  novelty. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18328).
- **PETS (Chua et al., 2018)** combines probabilistic model ensembles with
  uncertainty propagation in planning. Model disagreement is a useful candidate
  signal, but does not itself identify recoverability or the value of another
  measurement. [Primary paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2018/hash/3de568f8597b94bda53149c7d7f5958c-Abstract.html).

A defensible potential contribution is an experimental separation of
precommitment apparent success, independent verification failure, relevant versus
irrelevant history, and the cost of a warning-contingent investigation. None is
established here as a new algorithm or a demonstrated Nemotron learning effect.
Nemotron could later propose bounded physical hypotheses or explain a measured
warning; a distinct causal contribution would require a matched ablation.

## Reproducibility and resources

Existing Windows Python 3.11.9/NumPy 2.4.6 environment was reused, no installation
or new venv. All numerical fits are tiny CPU analyses of stored records with
one BLAS thread. The full grouped/dual-family experience analysis repeated
exactly under immutable publication, including identical stdout; one complete
repeat took 28.57 s. Exported V1 fold coefficients reproduce every saved V1
prediction exactly. No Linux/alternate NumPy bit-exact claim is made.

Twelve guards cover verifier/hidden-label exclusion, paid check provenance,
error/missing confirmation semantics, duplicate conflicts, held-out label
perturbation invariance for every model/control, physical and family blocking,
base-equal metrics, cluster pseudo-replication and the Beta reference. Independent
source checks validate 324 R0 hashes, 30 DI-1 hashes, 324 historical seed routes and
original observations, plus 358 logged charged stress4 checks at 20 s. All source-data hashes, predictions, folds, diagnostics
and compact evidence checksums accompany the reports. No positive-result retry,
new physical observation, model call or deployed learning system occurred.

**Actual new Cloud compute and Token Factory spend: $0.** No VM start occurred;
both VMs were API-checked STOPPED. Pre-existing stopped disks continue storage
charges, so this is not a claim that the account ledger/invoice is unchanged.
Final API check verified both STOPPED at 26 October 00:39:57 Oslo
(25 October 23:39:57 UTC), with unchanged compute-hour counters. The local ledger
rose $0.003 during the observed interval while disks remained allocated. Current Cloud
