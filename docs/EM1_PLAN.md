## Question and ordered decisions

H-EM1: correcting physical twin-response predictions using earlier experience
increases independently verified FULL recovery on unseen development bases at
equal plant/twin budgets. Predicting tool success directly is not this mechanism.
Prediction calibration alone cannot establish improved repair or experience causality.

1. G0: audit exact-intervention identity, physical conditions, seed roles,
   diagnostic chronology, independent support and decision relevance. Stop with
   NOT EVALUABLE / NO-GO if legitimate discrepancies or adequate decision support
   cannot be identified. Counts and precision, not the number of repeated seeds,
   determine feasibility. No fabricated pair labels.
2. If G0 supports a learner, freeze a small numerical residual model, grouped
   training/calibration and applicability rules before a new offline gate. Compare
   the original prediction, learned correction, sham experience and simple correction.
   Mechanism labels are evaluation strata only. Strictly separate historical
   training from evaluation, with base-held-out and family-held-out checks and
   prequential evaluation only if the record chronology permits it.
3. Only if offline repair-decision evidence and resource/power estimates justify
   it, register a fresh development generator, independent bases/seeds, primary
   B1−B0 FULL contrast, minimal useful effect, sham contrast, negative-transfer
   tolerance, budgets and PASS/FAIL/NOT EVALUABLE criteria before prospective outcomes.
   Do not borrow the sealed E-1 reserve. No tune-and-retry on evaluation outcomes.

## G0 data and measurement plan

Read only the explicitly named non-LLM v5 development files `fresh_A1c5.jsonl`
and `vs5_A1c.jsonl`. Their v5 diagnostic particles remain fixed during repair.
Reject outcome-assimilated records, ambiguous design identity, absent physical
ledger entries or duplicate episode sources. Raw files stay outside Git; record
exact file and source-row SHA-256 hashes.

The engine omits its cached per-seed predictions when serializing. Original
twin-CMA training scores use different seeds from plant robustness checks, and
per-tool RL-1 designs differ from screened candidates. Neither is a valid paired
residual. Recompute the existing eight-particle/nuisance cyclic ensemble on each
exact saved, checked design, using its original robustness seeds, variation,
duration and walking classifier. This is retrospective twin evaluation, not
prospectively logged forecasting or additional plant observation. Preserve every
result/error; no inference call is needed for this measurement.

The actual measured target is the aggregate walking count on 8 or 16 robustness
seeds. Individual plant seed outcomes are unavailable in the old log; do not
invent them. Full recovery of unsubmitted designs is also unavailable and must
not be inferred from walking fraction or a different design's verifier outcome.
Treat candidate availability as adaptively selected by the original pipeline,
not a complete random intervention panel.

G0 will quantify base-clustered bias/precision, variation across bases/families,
ranking discordance within the actually observed candidate sets, and missing
independent verification support. It will distinguish diagnostic model mismatch
from search failures and unresolved repair capability. Any later learner has
whitelisted numerical features only; instance/family identifiers and outcome
fields remain trusted routing/evaluation metadata outside its interface.

## Completed scope and stopping decision

All 804 distinct saved interventions were measured, preserving 866 original
log entries and 30 independent bases. Retrospective prediction bias exists,
but B0 already selects a measured robustness-walking maximizer in every observed
candidate set. Predictions/outcomes for earlier untried screened candidates and
FULL outcomes for 480 unsubmitted designs are absent. The ordered G0 requirement
of identifiable decision-relevant transfer is not met. This is a scoping
NO-GO, not a statistical rejection of H-EM1 or proof that correction cannot help.

Accordingly, the conditional learner stages above were not entered. Their
hyperparameters, applicability thresholds, chronological updates, B1/B2/B3 and
prospective effect/negative-transfer gates are **not registered or evaluated**.
Do not treat the exploratory G0 bootstrap or planning sensitivity as a positive
prospective result. A valid follow-up requires new decision-time paired evidence
and a separately frozen powered design; it must not repurpose the sealed reserve.
