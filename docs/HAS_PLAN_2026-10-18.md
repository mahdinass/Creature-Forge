## Context

The handoff concluded that the scientist loses to exhaustive screening (14–16 vs 23 of 40) because it stops early. A free re-analysis of the frozen traces (§1) suggests a more specific picture:
- stopping happens on statistically uninformative robustness evidence;
- repairs that act on the injected mechanism are *associated* with better verification;
- the old benchmark lets the robot's parameters and a fault-inverse catalogue stand in for causal reasoning.

**Goal:** Nemotron as an autonomous engineering scientist whose physical understanding and accumulated experience demonstrably improve engineering decisions.

**Approach (approved direction):** Architecture B, Hypothesis-as-Simulation. Nemotron writes executable physical hypotheses, designs discriminating experiments and repair strategies. A deterministic simulation-based Bayesian engine weighs the hypotheses against plant data. A learned amortised posterior (C) is a conditional component.

The evaluation is built to separate what the engine, Nemotron's hypotheses, its experiment and repair decisions, experience and learned models each contribute, under matched instruments and full plant + twin + token accounting.

### 1.2 Budget is not what makes repairs verify
- **Optimiser budget is weakly related to verification.** Given a repairing tool, P(FULL) is 0.31–0.53 across lineage rollouts with no trend. Exhaustive fails 15 times *with* a repairing tool and the full budget.
- **Policy-visible evidence is weak:**

### 1.3 Association between injected mechanism and verification
- Lineages including the injected mechanism's tool verify at 77/145 = 0.53, against 34/117 = 0.29 for other "repairing" tools. Within-instance: +0.22 [0.08, 0.35], 14 vs 4, sign p = 0.015.
- **Confounders that this analysis cannot exclude:**
  - arms choose tools based on evidence that also predicts difficulty;
  - the matching tool's search space was the one the bench was screened with (keep rule), so it may simply be the best-tuned subspace;
  - "injected mechanism" need not be the unique root cause;
  - several tools may be physically legitimate fixes.
- §5 therefore designs a **controlled** study of the value of mechanism knowledge.

### 1.4 First repair = injected mechanism
- Exhaustive 26/40, scientist + memory 17, learned 10, scientist 9/38, expert 8, random 7, one-shot 6.

## 3. Architecture B: Hypothesis-as-Simulation

### 3.1 Components

| Component | What it does | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| **Plant** | Nominal design (agent-visible) + hidden deviation (1–2 primitives) + hidden reality gap (per-instance nuisance on masses, damping, contact softness, actuator strength) | bench |
| **Twin** | Nominal-design simulator that can apply any DSL deviation | engine |
| **Deviation DSL** | Generic typed primitives: body attribute per segment/side; actuator strength per joint; joint damping/friction; sensor delay/bias/noise per signal group; controller-gain scale; ground friction/softness. At most 2 primitives with ranges. Defined independently of bench generators; identical for all arms | engine |
| **Belief engine** | Particles over (structure, parameters); synthetic likelihood from **paired same-seed twin simulations**; per-structure parameter fit; noise model from dev; EIG/disagreement over candidate experiments; posterior-robust repair (design optimised on the twin across posterior samples, then a short plant fine-tune); stopping by the validated mapping (§6) | engine (deterministic, numpy) |
| **Nemotron: Proposer** | Writes new hypotheses (DSL program + mechanism + causal chain + expected discriminating signature), given KEY FACTS, condition tests, posterior summary and **unexplained residuals** | LLM |
| **Nemotron: Experimenter** | Proposes candidate experiments, registers predictive distributions, chooses among the candidates (with engine-computed EIG visible) | LLM |
| **Nemotron: Engineer** | Repair strategy (subspace, physically reasoned initial design, n_train, budget) and predicted pass probability | LLM |
| **Nemotron: Consolidator** | After dev investigations: executable mechanism dossiers (template, signature conditions, discriminating experiment, validated strategy, failure cases), with deterministic support statistics | LLM |
| **Learned posterior (C, conditional)** | MDN over DSL primitives/parameters from observation + condition-test summaries + nominal features; trained on simulated deviations over dev bases, **excluding held-out primitives** | learned |

### 3.2 Learned components (question 5)

| Learn? | Component | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| **Yes** | amortised deviation posterior (conditional, Tier B) | identification; transferable across morphologies; cheap simulated data |
| **Yes** | likelihood noise model | needed for honest likelihoods |
| **Yes, small** | **robustness-to-verification mapping** (§6) | the stopping rule needs a validated calibration |
| **No** | intervention-effect regression | the twin plus identified deviation is the effect model (§1.6) |
| **No** | applicability classifier | replaced by posterior weight |
| **Deferred** | RL experiment policy (DAD/CAASL) | premature |
| **Deferred** | learned dynamics model (GNN/TD-MPC2) | no GPU; MuJoCo is exact |
| **Deferred** | fine-tuning Nemotron | availability unverified |

## 4. Benchmark v2: Hidden-Damage Bench (HDB), defined independently of comparative outcomes

### 4.1 Population (fixed before any comparative run)

**Mechanism families** chosen from an engineering failure taxonomy (actuation, sensing, mass properties, contact/environment, geometry/structure, control), not from any arm's performance, and organised as symptom-twins:

| Twin pair | Members | Split |
|---|---|---|
| T1 balance instability | feedback-gain loss vs **sensor latency** | dev / held-out |
| T2 lean | encoder pitch bias vs payload COM offset | dev / dev |
| T3 weakness | global actuator weakness vs **joint damping wear** | dev / held-out |
| T4 slip vs support | low ground friction vs short feet | dev / dev |

- Singles: one-side actuator weakness (dev), cadence fault (dev), heavy payload (dev), **leg-length asymmetry** (held-out; possibly unrecoverable by the toolset).
- **Novel compositions** (held-out): pairs of dev primitives that are individually tolerable.

**Severity grid:** fixed on a physics scale (log-spaced magnitudes per primitive, set from first principles and nominal-model sensitivity), not tuned on any arm.

**Bases:**
- About 100 new verified walkers from a new campaign. Morphology clusters are split dev 35–40 / test 55–60 before any HDB run.
- Nearest-dev distance is reported.
- The old 40 held-out instances are never reused.

### 4.2 Eligibility: physical criteria only
- Valid: in bounds, no simulation errors, never clipped.
- **The plant fails:** ≤ 2/8 check seeds walking. That is the definition of a failure case.
- The healthy base is a verified walker.
- **No criterion uses any policy's result.** The earlier "A0 ≤ 80%" condition is removed.

### 4.3 Transparent oracle strata (labels, not filters)
Every eligible instance gets:
- **R/U label** from a fixed, published oracle procedure: posterior-robust repair given the *true* deviation structure, at the evaluation plant budget. R = FULL under `full_recovery_v1`, U = not. A wide union-space oracle at 4× budget is also recorded.
- **Ambiguity label** from the identifiability study (§4.4): instances whose injected deviation is not separable from another structure by the available instruments at budget.
- **Response row:** the verified (FULL) outcome of each intervention family at the evaluation budget. Published, never used for selection.

### 4.4 Identifiability study (before any comparative run; simulation only, cheap)
- **Method:** for each twin pair and family on dev bases, simulate plants (with reality gap) and compute instrument outputs:
  - observation;
  - condition tests: floor-friction test, slow-gait probe, push test, suspended joint sine-sweep (tracking, phase lag, saturation);
  - counterfactuals;
  - robustness check.
- **Report:**
  - cross-validated classification accuracy, confusion matrices and KL/Fisher separation for (a) observation only, (b) + condition tests, (c) + counterfactuals, as a function of plant rollouts spent;
  - **observational equivalence classes**: deviations the instruments cannot separate;
  - **intervention distinguishability**: paired twin-plant effect of each twin's fix, i.e. does the wrong twin's fix measurably fail or harm.
- **Use:** families that are not separable stay in the population as a declared **ambiguous stratum**. They are not dropped and not redesigned to favour anyone.
- **Scoring:** identification is scored against the injected deviation's equivalence class, never as a unique root cause.

### 4.5 Sampling
- Stratified random sampling from all eligible instances, with fixed seed and quotas per family declared in the protocol.
- The primary population includes R, U and ambiguous instances. McNemar uses only discordant pairs, so U instances cost power, not validity.
- Per-stratum results are secondary.

### 4.6 Instruments (all arms identical; one plant budget of 700 rollouts; twin cap 2,800 = 4× plant, fixed *a priori*)
- `observe`
- parametric counterfactuals on design parameters (no fault-inverse catalogue)
- condition tests (listed in §4.4)
- `repair(subspace, budget, n_train ∈ {4, 8})` with tool ratio ranges ×0.25–4, plus new tools: per-joint torque; sensor filtering/offset compensation
- robustness check (n ∈ {8, 16} seeds from a policy-visible "robust" namespace)
- twin queries (counted)
- **Verification:** `full_recovery_v1`, hidden, against the faulty plant, unchanged.

**Freeze rule:** families, primitives, severity grid, instruments and the eligibility code are committed and frozen **after the identifiability study and before the first comparative dev run**. Any later change is a dated amendment with a stated reason (e.g. a bug). Changes are never motivated by arm results.

## 5. Controlled study: does knowing the mechanism add engineering value? (Mechanism-Knowledge Value, MKV; non-LLM)

**Design:**
- Each instance receives every condition: within-instance, paired, same plant budget, same seeds, same optimiser, same twin cap.
- Conditions differ **only** in the mechanism information given to the posterior-robust repair procedure.

| Condition | Information given |
|---|---|
| K0 | none: generic union-space repair, plus the twin with nominal model only |
| K1 | true deviation **structure** (parameters unknown, identified by the engine) |
| K2 | true structure **and** parameters |
| K3 | the **symptom-twin's** structure (wrong diagnosis) |
| K4 | the engine's own identified posterior (no oracle) |

**Estimands** (paired, per stratum):

| Contrast | Measures |
|---|---|
| K1 − K0 | value of correct mechanism knowledge |
| K2 − K1 | value of quantitative identification |
| K3 − K0 | harm of a wrong mechanism |
| K4 − K0 | value of the engine's actual inference |

**Run:**
- Dev first.
- Then registered on the test set as **confirmatory study C-MKV**, separate from the AI comparison: primary K1 > K0, one-sided exact McNemar on FULL. If it fails, the premise "diagnosis matters" is not supported on this benchmark, and that is reported.
- Cost: non-LLM, about 3.5k rollout-eq per condition per instance.

This replaces the exploratory association of §1.3 as the basis for any claim that causal knowledge matters.

## 6. Stopping rule: a validated mapping from visible evidence to verification

**Problem:** a Beta posterior on short trials is not P(pass `full_recovery_v1`). Verification also needs VERIFIED_IMPROVEMENT and a 20 s confirmation with speed and no-dragging criteria.

**Calibration dataset (dev only):**
- Designs produced on dev instances by every non-LLM arm, the MKV conditions and LLM dev pilots, plus deliberately diverse repairs at varying budgets.
- For each design, record:
  - **visible evidence:** train walking/fitness, robustness-check counts (n = 8 / 16), stress-test result, twin posterior-predictive pass probability, posterior entropy;
  - **true outcome:** `full_recovery_v1` on dev verification seeds.
- Target: about 1,500 designs, about 0.25 M rollouts.

**Mapping:**
- Candidates: logistic / isotonic models of P(FULL | evidence).
- Validation: base-clustered cross-validation.
- Metrics: Brier, log loss, ECE, reliability diagrams, AUROC.
- Comparison: against the naive Beta-posterior reading, shown explicitly as a baseline.

**Stopping policies compared on dev** (replay where possible, live where not; each also scored on rollouts used):

| Policy | Rule |
|---|---|
| fixed-spend-all | the exhaustive style |
| train 4/4 | the old scientist |
| robust k/n | e.g. ≥ 15/16 |
| successive-halving over candidate designs | — |
| calibrated | stop when mapped P(FULL) ≥ τ or the expected value of continuing < cost; τ chosen on dev |

**Freeze:** the mapping and τ are frozen at registration. Calibration on test is a registered secondary (S4), never refitted.

### 7.3 Process-level attribution by deterministic replay (no plant cost, twin compute reported)
Plant data are recorded, so the engine can be re-run on identical data:
- **Hypothesis contribution:**
  - posterior with vs without Nemotron-proposed particles (and dossier particles);
  - measured as log-score of the injected equivalence class, time to the first particle in the true class, and marginal-likelihood share of LLM particles.
- **Experiment contribution:** at every step, engine-estimated EIG of Nemotron's chosen experiment vs the engine's best candidate and vs the median candidate (information-efficiency ratio).
- **Repair contribution:** at the same posterior, twin-estimated mapped P(FULL) of Nemotron's repair strategy vs the engine default. Live plant outcome comes only from the A3 vs A3hx arm contrast.

### 7.4 Predictive distributions (§8) at identical information states
Three forecasts per registered experiment:
- **N**: Nemotron's direct predictive distribution;
- **E**: engine-only posterior predictive, replayed without LLM particles;
- **E+N**: engine posterior predictive with Nemotron particles.

Contrasts:
- E+N vs E: does Nemotron improve the physical belief;
- N vs E: is Nemotron's intuition competitive with the engine.

Weaker references (no-change, climatology, kNN, nominal twin) are reported in addition.

### 7.5 Hypotheses (registered before the confirmatory test-set build)
- **Primary (fixed now; identical in every protocol variant):** P: FULL(A3) > FULL(A1), one-sided exact McNemar, α = 0.05. This isolates Nemotron's total contribution on top of the engine with everything else matched.
- **Separate confirmatory study C-MKV:** K1 > K0 (§5), its own α = 0.05.
- **Secondary (Holm):**
  - S1 A3h > A1 (hypotheses);
  - S2 A3 > A0 (vs the old champion procedure);
  - S3 E+N log-score / CRPS > E;
  - S4 calibration of the frozen stopping mapping on test (Brier skill > 0 vs a constant forecast);
  - S5 A1 > A1f.
  - Tier B (only if registered): S6 A4 > A3, S7 A2 > A1; negative-transfer rate on twin instances ≤ the naive "nearest dossier's repair" policy; corrupted-dossier override rate.
- **Descriptive:** all other contrasts in §7.2, process replays, per-stratum (R/U/ambiguous, held-out families, novel compositions), compute and cost.
- **Sample:** N = 160 fresh test instances.

### 7.6 Protocol variants (declared now, chosen by a dated rule)
- **P-main** (Tier B complete by 25 Oct 18:00): all arms.

## 8. Measurement framework for physical prediction
- **Targets per experiment:**
  - walking count / n;
  - time_upright, capture_margin_mean, pitch_mean, step_freq, slip_fraction, max saturation;
  - robustness-check fraction;
  - mapped P(FULL).
- **Forecast format:** quantiles 5/25/50/75/95 for continuous targets; a probability vector for counts; a probability for pass. Registered before execution (existing event-sequence pattern).
- **Scores:** CRPS (pinball approximation), interval score at 50% and 90%, ranked probability score / log score, Brier; PIT histograms and coverage.
- **Skill** relative to E (main reference) and the weaker references. Base-clustered bootstrap CIs.
- **Noise floor:** seed-to-seed and reality-gap variance from dev replaces fixed tolerances.
- **Within-episode feedback:** running coverage is shown to Nemotron; any change in coverage error is descriptive.

## 11. Integrity and anti-leakage safeguards
1. **Visibility:**
   - agents see only the nominal design and outputs of the charged instruments;
   - never plant parameters, deviation, reality gap, family, strata labels or response rows;
   - `policy_view_v2` is tested.
2. **Sealed held-out primitives:**
   - generators live in a sealed module, never imported by agent-facing code;
   - excluded from dossiers, learned-model training and prompt examples;
   - prompts are checked by grep for family names.
3. **Fresh morphologies:** test bases are from a new campaign and never used in development; the old held-out set is never reused.
4. **Seed roles:**
   - new campaign namespaces;
   - verification seeds are never visible;
   - twin and robustness seeds come from separate namespaces, never validate/confirm.
5. **Outcome-independent bench:**
   - families, severity grid, eligibility and strata are frozen before comparative runs;
   - stratified random sampling with declared quotas;
   - no outcome-based selection;
   - amendments are dated and justified.
6. **Registration before the test build:**
   - one analysis;
   - resume-only reruns;
   - checksums;
   - the frozen manifest is extended;
   - the stopping mapping is frozen.
7. **Compute parity:**
   - plant and twin rollouts are ledgered per episode, against identical caps;
   - tokens and dollars are reported;
   - unused twin capacity is reported, not reallocated.
8. **Attribution:**
   - engine or optimiser recoveries are never credited to Nemotron;
   - Nemotron's contribution is claimed only through §7.2–7.4 contrasts;
   - association analyses (§1) stay labelled exploratory.
9. **Operations:**
   - no tuning on test;
   - secret guard on every record;
   - pre-run auth check;
   - no credential changes during runs;
   - API stop after every block.
