## Reliability gate frozen before new outcomes

Stage A passes only when ALL hold:

- Exactly 30 new error-free verifier records, identities and seeds match.
- At least **12/15** bases have the SAME binary FULL/non-FULL across all three
  panels (original + two new), i.e. no more than three discordant bases.
- Pairwise FULL agreement >=**85%** over 45 within-base panel pairs.
- Pairwise agreement >=**80%** separately for independent improvement and
  validation-walking requirements.
- At least **three persistent FULL and three persistent non-FULL** bases.

These are engineering feasibility tolerances: at most20% observed unstable
cases and support for both classes. They are not confidence guarantees or a
population reliability estimate. Pair observations are correlated. Report
Wilson 95% binomial REFERENCE for 15 all-panel agreements, exact per-base
patterns, all statuses, component-specific agreement and conditional observation
counts, family/cohort heterogeneity and requirement-level changes.

If instability fails the agreement thresholds: NO-GO for treating a single
verifier panel as a stable case-specific learning label in this tested context.
This does not reject all experience learning. If only class support/completeness
is inadequate, or Stage B cannot fit, conclude INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE for H-VEL2.
If gate passes and Stage B fits, continue to a separately frozen 30-new-lineage
diagnostic panel. A larger-research GO additionally requires useful physical
signal and positive relevant-history increment over BOTH V1 and V3; A alone
cannot yield GO. Do not claim recovery improvement without matched independent
FULL alternatives and equal charged budgets. No further study starts by default.
