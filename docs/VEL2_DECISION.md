# VEL-2 decision — NO-GO for the tested stable-label prerequisite

**VEL-2: NO-GO** for advancing this frozen design to Stage B using one full
verifier panel as a sufficiently stable case-specific FULL/non-FULL learning
label. This is the preregistered measurement-readiness decision, **not a
rejection of experience learning or H-VEL2 in general**. The broader predictive
and decision-value claims remain **INSUFFICIENT EVIDENCE**, because their
prospective comparison was correctly not entered.

All30 new full-verifier measurements on15 known independent development
lineages completed without simulator errors. Only9/15 labels agreed across
all three panels (required12), and FULL pairwise agreement was73.3% (required85%).
Seven bases stayed FULL, two stayed non-FULL, and six changed. Persistent
failure support also fell short (two versus three). No thresholds, seeds,
cohort, labels or verification requirements were changed to reverse the gate.

Independent improvement was consistent on all three panels for all15 bases;
14 always passed and one always failed. Label changes arose from validation
walking, confirmation walking and contact, rather than improvement. This
distinction prevents an unsupported blanket claim that VEL-1's predominant
improvement-failure target is unstable. The fixed R0 sample is too small and
different to resolve that claim or generalize to new lineages.

No new V0/V1/V2/V3 accuracy contrast, transferable memory benefit, useful
warning-contingent investigation, better FULL recovery, online learning or
Nemotron contribution was demonstrated. Stage A is a measurement-validity
study. Historical VEL-1 scores are unchanged and are not rebranded as VEL-2
results. Additional available money does not override the Stage A gate.
