# EM-1 decision — NO-GO for further implementation now

**EM-1 gate: NOT EVALUABLE. Do not advance to a full online learning experiment
or a Nemotron-directed correction system on the current evidence.** G0 found a
physical prediction discrepancy but did not establish a decision-relevant,
transferable recovery target. This is an identifiability/scoping NO-GO, not a
rejection of H-EM1 or a claim that all model correction is futile.

The decisive findings are:

1. On 30 development bases, 804 exact interventions and 11,632 matched seed
   responses, observed robustness walking exceeds the reconstructed model by
   5.18 percentage points, descriptive base-cluster interval [1.58,8.97]. Model
   error exists; the missing original prediction cache can be repaired by valid
   retrospective evaluation on saved designs.
2. B0's actual final choice already maximizes measured robustness walking within
   every one of its 324 observed candidate sets. Correcting this late walking
   forecast supplies no demonstrated information beyond the plant checks B0
   already paid for. Earlier untried candidates, search trajectories and their
   matched predicted/actual responses are not identifiable from those sets.
3. FULL is unknown for 480 alternatives. Even the simple raw audit ranker's
   86 changed submissions have no FULL labels. Better walking prediction is not
   enough to identify better full recovery, particularly improvement over the
   faulty robot and 20-second confirmation. Unknown outcomes cannot become
   failures or successes for convenience.
4. No justified powered prospective design follows from this evidence within
   the present budget. Thirty bases do not supply the proposed new policy's
   discordance rate; optimistic effect/variance assumptions cannot substitute for
   a comparable pilot. The task stopped rather than spending the remaining cap.

Accordingly, no B1 learner, sham B2, statistical B3 or Nemotron contribution was
tested. No success, null effect, absence of negative transfer or experience
causality is claimed for them. A global additive bias correction alone preserves
rank order before clipping and is not a repair-learning mechanism. Training a
new tool-success predictor on available verifier labels would repeat RL-1's
question rather than test physical model correction.

A valid next development investigation would first create **decision-time
paired evidence**, not another policy from these missing counterfactuals:

- Log exact proposed candidate designs, immutable pre-observation twin models,
  hypothesis weights/uncertainty, seeds, conditions and predicted physical
  quantities before any plant result. Preserve intermediate screened candidates
  and search history, including candidates never selected by B0.
- Use a prospectively fixed, physically bounded intervention panel or randomized
  exploration allocation to obtain comparable plant responses. Record per-seed
  speed, displacement, contact and stability as well as walking, with a budget
  matched comparator. This separates model discrepancy from search/capability.
- Keep diagnostic probes and historical experience strictly causal. Fit on
  designated training bases only; calibrate support/uncertainty/abstention inside
  grouped training folds. Test genuinely unseen bases and family transfer without
  treating family holdout as removing mechanisms from the twin's library.
- Evaluate a small non-LLM residual baseline first, with sham experience and
  comparable FULL outcomes for selected alternatives. Add Nemotron only for a
  concrete hypothesis/probe/applicability role, preserving validated structured
  outputs, identity/prompts/tokens and equal information/interaction budgets.
- Before new prospective outcomes, freeze B0/B1/B2/B3, minimum useful FULL effect,
  negative-transfer tolerance, independent generator/seeds, base-clustered power,
  analysis and PASS/FAIL/NOT EVALUABLE rules. A separate confirmatory study or
  sealed evaluation still requires owner authorization.

This is a data/experimental-design requirement, not a request for more money to
continue the same unsupported run. No further paid work or deployment is
scheduled. Both VMs are STOPPED; conservative additional Cloud estimate $1.097,
0.517 paid hours, Token Factory $0. Evidence and limitations: `EM1_RESULTS.md`,
`EM1_FEASIBILITY.md` and the dedicated EM-1 result manifests. Earlier negative
