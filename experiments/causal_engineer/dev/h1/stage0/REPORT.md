# H1 Stage 0 — read-only scientific feasibility audit

26 October 2026. Evidence pinned to `e8d5fa6730ecf6d2db67021a9d3d0f08cce03310`.

**Executive verdict: NOT EVALUABLE for proceeding directly to a prospective H1 AI-selector pilot.**
The present evidence identifies some standardized tool-level actionability and
a cheap physical signal, but does not identify final-selector regret under the
actual existing candidate availability, information and resource budgets. It
also does not establish incremental experience value. This is not a universal
NO-GO for selection. A new learner is premature; the next useful measurement is
counterfactual evaluation of already-generated, exact alternatives.

Within RL-1's specified matrix endpoint, capability/search is the larger observed
bottleneck: 81 of 101 engine-top-two failures have no FULL tool anywhere in the
ten-tool matrix. Only20 could possibly be rescued by reranking that matrix.
This is evidence about that budget/action space/one optimizer realization, not
physical irreparability or the failure decomposition of the actual A1 pipeline.

## Actionability: what can and cannot be identified

RL-1 has207 instances with10 unique designs, three with9, and one with8.
All130 instances containing any FULL also contain non-FULL alternatives; there
are no all-FULL instances. Both the130 mixed instances and81 no-FULL instances
span all18 bases. There are381 FULL tool cells total. These establish observed
single-panel outcome diversity, not precise candidate reliability.

All ten repairs together actually use **2,904 plant rollouts per instance**
(individual cells284 or292, capped at300). This excludes acquisition of the
historical diagnosis/twin screen context and independent evaluation costs.
Treating the matrix as a final candidate bag while charging the incumbent only
its original700-rollout cap would be an unfair comparison. Historical matrix
tool ranking is a useful standardized allocation endpoint, not actual A1's
warm-started/chained/robustly screened final choice.

R0 provides the directly relevant final choice sets: every unit contains exactly
two distinct screened candidates. Replaying walking/survival/fitness ordering
recovers all324 recorded choices, including135 choices of second-ranked training
candidate after its selection-seed check. There are13,392 recorded portfolio
proposals before deduplication (27–69 per unit, median28); these counts are not
unique fully verified alternatives or all CMA population samples.

Exact joining uses instance, physical body/vector hash, validation/confirmation
seed lists and variation conditions. It recovers the unchosen label in **one**
of324 screens. That pair is unit `3d4d54fb85919ba8d699c54d`,
`hdb-v1-gen-0037:pitch_sensor_bias:4`, sequential/DO1 replicate0. The actual
choice is FULL; its alternative is PARTIAL because validation walking fails.
Both had selection walking7/8, survival8/8; incumbent fitness was higher.

Thus there is **zero identified missed-FULL alternative**, but only ONE fully
labelled pair. This is absence of counterfactual evidence, not zero selector
regret. There are157 known-FULL screens,167 chosen-non-FULL screens and323
unchosen slot labels unknown, corresponding to320 distinct missing candidate
identities. The mathematical oracle bound is correspondingly broad: at least
157/324, potentially324/324 if every unresolved failed screen had a good
alternative. That upper bound is not an attainable policy estimate.

Every one of648 screen entries can be recovered without simulation:623 saved
search winners and25 numerical tail reconstructions. All648 hashes match,
yielding640 exact designs. No approximate design is substituted. A cross-check
of fresh-v5 actual repair bags found310 alternative checked-design episode
entries, with zero physical matches to RL-1 labels; matrix labels do not repair
the actual engine's missing FULL counterfactuals either.

R0's union across all18 units has some FULL on15/18 cases and none on3/18.
That union cost54,000 policy rollouts/case and combines action spaces, objectives
and optimizer randomness. It is an expensive retrospective capability oracle,
not an original legal choice set. Three of12 historically failed cases still
have no observed success; this does not certify an optimal search or impossibility.

There is no single interchangeable incumbent. Historical v5 checks actual
robust walking and submits its best fraction/size/index evidence, with calibrated

must freeze the exact baseline version and include these existing improvement-
aware rules; presenting them as a new AI contribution would be misleading.
The R0 runner does not persist the full DO1 component/per-seed log for every
selection measurement, so arbitrary alternative requirement-aware rules cannot
be replayed from its scalar alone.

## Failure requirements, timing and leakage

The unchanged full_recovery_v1 requires24 matched10s validation seeds with
VERIFIED_IMPROVEMENT and >=80% walking; only then24 further20s confirmation
seeds requiring >=80% walking, adequate median upright speed and every-seed
contact within the existing limit. The improvement sign test and minimum
paired0.5m effect are separate from walking or survival. Any simulator ERROR
is inconclusive, not a physical failure label.

R0 observed requirement failures overlap:48 improvement,112 validation walking,
27 confirmation walking,7 confirmation contact,0 median speed. Confirmation
does not run in134 cases. The sole completely labelled pair differs in validation
walking; no other within-pair cause contrast is identified.

RL-1's compressed statuses and validation fractions permit limited logical
routing, not detailed confirmation reconstruction:1,127 NOT_RECOVERED imply
failed improvement;1,447 have failed validation walking;129 PARTIAL candidates
pass validation walking and therefore failed at least one confirmation condition.
Confirmation is inferred unexecuted in1,600 cells. Which confirmation component
failed is unknown. Among4/4 train walkers:266 improvement failures,326 validation-
walking failures and106 conditional confirmation failures; categories overlap.
Missing confirmation is never counted as observed long-horizon failure.

VEL-2's6/15 candidate-label flips came from walking/contact, while improvement
decisions were stable. Its one improvement-failing base is insufficient to
estimate that target's reliability. Shared original panels across many R0
policies do not create repeat-panel reliability estimates. Independent disjoint
panels vary sampled conditions; a three-panel binary majority is not a precise
per-candidate reliability estimate.

Legal predecision information: exact design, four-train/eight-or16-screen
walking/survival/fitness, already charged matched baseline/displacement,
stress observations when acquired, pre-observation twin predictions, diagnostic
posterior, candidate availability, remaining budget and accessible frozen
history. Source call order supports timing, although R0/RL-1 lack fine-grained
wall-clock event journals and serialize final records after verification.

Illegal features: validation walking_frac, verifier scores or seed-level rows,
FULL/partial status, failure reasons, confirmation-run flag, true hidden plant/
family/severity labels, post-outcome posterior updates, hindsight best candidate
or EM-1 forecasts relabelled as originally available. Candidate IDs/hashes serve
identity/grouping, not memorized outcome lookup. Twin tool screens and separately
optimized RL-1 designs are different physical objects, not matched prediction residuals.

## Experience headroom

The existing RL-1 learned memory failed its registered top-two gate:108/211
versus engine110/211, despite useful probability information. Its published
secondary top-one result is88 versus78; that deserves honest reporting but
does not establish a final-selector or experience-over-physical-only effect.
The new train-count rule's89 is not a fair same-information pre-generation
competitor to that learned tool ranker either.

VEL-1 physical/state Brier.1769, relevant residual memory.1772 and shuffled
history.1757 supply no established history increment. Prediction of a chosen
candidate is not a test of choosing another candidate. EM-1's measured model
error arrives without identifiable decision regret; DI-1's random-path rescue
mixes investigation and search effects. E-1's primary contrast remains
inconclusive; only its published summaries were consulted. No cross-study
vote counting, pooling or H1 prospective-evidence claim is justified.

Plausible future opportunity: requirement-specific margins may discriminate
walking/contact/improvement failures among otherwise tied candidates. There
is currently no evidence that relevant historical failure examples add value
beyond those physical measurements and a correctly budgeted conventional rule.

## Smallest next measurement — proposal only, not launched

First complete the320 exact missing R0 candidate labels under original matched
conditions, freezing identities beforehand. This isolates alternative actionability
without new healthy lineages, search or a learner. It remains a historical
development diagnostic, not prospective H1 validation. To examine measurement
uncertainty, add two fresh shared, disjoint verifier panels for ALL640 candidates,
not just favorable/misranked ones. Keep all324 screens and18 base groups.
One-panel completion alone cannot resolve reliability.

Estimate within-screen candidate differences with paired conditions and whole-base
uncertainty; use a hierarchical panel/requirement model with partial pooling,
reporting sensitivity to its assumptions. Preserve conditional confirmation
censoring explicitly; do not multiply marginal component probabilities as if
independent. Three panels are initial variance measurements, not exact candidate
probabilities. Extra panels would need a separate frozen allocation/resource rule.

If this shows little actionable regret, stop H1 selection and prioritize controller/
action-space expressiveness, coordinated repair and search/objective alignment
on development mechanisms. If differences exist but predecision rules cannot
predict their direction, the learner remains NOT EVALUABLE; collect targeted
matched non-verifier responses rather than fit to verifier outcomes.

A later prospective pilot, if independently justified, should target
E_base,panel[FULL(selector(G,legal_features)) - FULL(current_selector(G,legal_features))]
for an explicitly frozen common candidate generator G and equal total charged
plant/twin/screening budgets. A common-generator result is not automatically an
end-to-end gain over the old policy's different early-stopping/generation path.
Generate truly new bodies from independently seeded creature_gen.sample_body,
not historical-parent perturbations; freeze attempt stream, nominal qualification,
fault checks and exclusions before outcomes. Require at least two distinct legal
candidates per decision; inadequate availability is a measured failure, not a
post-outcome exclusion. New-lineage qualification yield is currently unmeasured.

Use separate training and evaluation lineages (plus dual base/family exclusion
where support permits), physical-only/incumbent rules first, then relevant and
matched shuffled-history controls with identical architecture, features, data
volume and compute. Predictions/choices must be committed before independent
verifier panels. A later experience bank cannot contain evaluation-lineage or
future-panel outcomes. Report FULL and requirement effects, regret, false
warnings, negative-transfer cases and cost; abstention alone is not recovery.

Proposed future gates, NOT retrospective preregistration: actionability must
exceed a useful oracle-regret threshold after measurement uncertainty; legal
physical rules must improve decisions on separate bases; an experience selector
must then beat BOTH incumbent/physical-only and sham history at equal budgets.
For example,5pp could be a useful net FULL effect and5pp a tolerated harmful
switch rate, but the owner/scientific protocol must freeze values and power
before data collection. PASS requires all applicable lower/upper bounds;
FAIL requires sufficiently precise evidence of inadequate benefit/excess harm;
otherwise NOT EVALUABLE. No data-driven threshold changes.

Thirty evaluation bases would be exploratory. A conventional paired-binary
planning approximation at two-sided5%,80% power needs about150–307 independent
evaluation bases for10pp benefit if discordance is.2–.4, or621–1,248 for5pp.
Those discordances are assumptions, not identified from these records. Repeat-
panel/requirement outcomes need a dedicated clustered measurement-error power
analysis and possibly more bases; required history-training N is unknown.
